Massing and model by face

Masses are conceptual volumes for early design: their floors give areas and volumes per storey, and walls, floors, roofs and curtain systems can be made on their faces.

See it in the Massing and site sample (two masses with floors, modelled by face), with its walkthrough.

Making a mass

  1. Draw closed loops of model lines (Architecture › Model Line): one loop for a block, or a loop on each of several levels (with as many corners each) for a lofted form.
  2. Select them and click Massing & Site › In-Place Mass In-Place Mass. Name it; a single loop asks for the height.

Masses are drawn translucent. Extrusions, lofts, revolves, blends and sweeps can be made with mass.create and mass.add_form; mass.set_form stretches an extrusion.

Mass floors

Select a mass and click Mass Floors, then tick the levels. Each ticked level that crosses the mass (not at its very top) gets a mass floor with its area, perimeter, the volume up to the next floor and the exterior surface of that band (the top floor's includes the roof). They follow the mass as it changes; a level above it gets a floor once the mass reaches past it. mass.set_floors, mass.floors

Model by face

Select a mass, click a tool, and tick its faces in the list (named like form0.side2, with the way each faces):

Wall by FaceWalls on upright faces, along the face's bottom edge and as high as it, their exterior finish face on the face.
Floor by FaceFloors on the mass floors.
Roof by FaceA roof slab on each upward face.
Curtain SystemA curtain grid with panels and mullions on the faces; one mullion where two faces meet.

These remember their faces but do not follow the mass by themselves: select them and click Update to Face after changing the mass. wall.create_by_face, floor.create_by_mass_floor, roof.create_by_face, curtainsystem.create_by_face, element.update_to_face, mass.select_dependents

Not yet available: the conceptual design environment (X-Ray, dragging faces and edges), voids in masses, Place Mass, Show Mass modes and joined masses.
