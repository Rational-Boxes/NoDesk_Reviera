Remote analysis subscriptions

A subscription to a remote analysis service lets heavy analyses run on that service's computers. Reviera ships with none and connects to no service until you add one.

Adding a subscription

Analyze › Remote Analysis (also on Manage › Settings and the File menu) lists your subscriptions. Add… asks for a name, the service's address (it must start with https://) and the API key and secret your service gave you. You can paste them as one key:secret in the key field and leave Secret empty. Reviera tests the connection before saving; if the test fails, it says why and nothing is saved.

Edit… changes the name, address, key or advanced settings, or replaces the secret (the stored secret is never shown; leave the field empty to keep it). Remove deletes the subscription and its secret. Jobs already on the service keep running there, but can only be fetched after you add the subscription again. Set as Default chooses the subscription used when none is named, for every job kind or for one.

Status and warnings

The list shows each subscription's status (Active, Trial, Suspended, Expired, Key refused, Unreachable, Secret needed or Not checked), plan, usage, and when the period ends. Under the list, the selected one shows what is left of the allowance, the job kinds it offers and its limits. Refresh asks the service for the current status. Test Connection signs in afresh first. Reviera warns when the period ends within 7 days and when 90 % of the allowance is used.

When the service refuses the key, Reviera does not keep trying it: change the key or secret with Edit…, or ask your administrator.

How the key and secret are kept

  • The secret is kept in your system's keychain (the Secret Service on Linux, Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows). If there is no keychain, it is kept only until Reviera closes, and the subscription then shows Secret needed.
  • The key and secret are sent only to the subscription's own address, over a secure connection, to sign in. Every other request carries a short-lived token instead, which Reviera renews before it runs out.
  • The secret never appears in your settings files, in results, or in logs. AI agents and MCP clients can list subscriptions and read their status, but cannot add, change, test or remove them.

Self-hosted services

A service with its own certificate authority can be trusted by giving that CA's PEM file under Advanced: CA file. A pin (the SHA-256 of the service's certificate, 64 hex digits) accepts exactly that certificate instead. Development allows a service on this computer at http://localhost, for people writing a service. The service's side of the connection is described in schemas/compute/reviera/compute/v1/subscription.proto.
