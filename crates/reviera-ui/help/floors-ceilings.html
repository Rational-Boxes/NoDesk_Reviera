Floors and ceilings

Floors and ceilings are slabs drawn from a sketch of their outline. A ceiling can also be placed in one click inside a room, and then follows the room's walls.

To create a floor

  1. Open the plan of the floor's level. Architecture › Build › Floor (FL). Floor
  2. The sketch starts with Pick Walls: click the walls round the floor (on the side the floor goes), or hover one and press Tab for the whole chain. Or draw the outline with Line, Rectangle, Arc or Circle. See Sketch mode.
  3. Finish Edit Mode.

A round floor can be picked from curved walls (their inner faces become arcs) or drawn with Circle, as in the Observatory.

Floor properties

Level, Height Offset From LevelWhere the floor's top is.
Area, Perimeter, Volume, ThicknessComputed, read only.
TypeFloor types set the thickness. Choose one, or Duplicate Type….

To change the outline, drag its grips in a plan, or use Modify | Floors › Edit Boundary. Modify | Floors › Guard Edges puts railings along its open edges (see Railings). Floors are saved as IfcSlab. floor.create, floor.edit_boundary, floor.set_level

To place a ceiling

  1. Open a plan of the level, a ceiling plan being best. Architecture › Build › Ceiling. Ceiling
  2. The Place Ceiling tab offers Automatic Ceiling (the default) and Sketch Ceiling.
  3. With Automatic Ceiling, click inside a room enclosed by walls: the ceiling fills it. Click more rooms; Esc ends.
  4. With Sketch Ceiling, sketch its boundary as for a floor and finish.

An automatic ceiling's edges follow the walls' finish faces, round rooms included, and move with the walls. Ceilings sit 2600 mm above their level by default and have types. Select one and use Modify | Ceilings › Edit Boundary to change its outline. Ceilings are saved as IfcCovering (CEILING). ceiling.create_auto, ceiling.create, ceiling.edit_boundary

Ceilings that follow the roof

Under a roof, a ceiling can follow the roof's underside instead of being flat: a vaulted ceiling under a pitched roof, a barrel under an arched roof, a dome under a dome. Set the Ceiling tool's Shape to Follow Roof in Properties before clicking a room, or select a ceiling and set its Shape. The ceiling takes the shape of the roof over it, face by face; any part the roof does not cover stays flat at its height. It changes with the roof. Such ceilings are saved with faceted bodies. ceiling.set_shape, ceiling.create_auto (follow_roof)

A following ceiling's area and the stair headroom check still use its flat height.

Ceiling plans

View › Ceiling Plan makes a reflected ceiling plan of a level, cut at 2300 mm and looking up. See Views.

Openings in floors and ceilings

A stair offers to open the floors and ceilings above it where headroom is short. Those openings can then be reshaped with their grips. See Openings.

Slab edges

A slab edge sweeps a profile along a floor's edges: an edge beam, a thickened edge or a curb. Select a floor, then Modify | Floors › Slab Edge Slab Edge and click its edges one after another; they form one mitred run (all round, a closed one). In Properties set its Width (away from the slab), Depth (down), the profile offsets, and Flip Horizontal (under the slab) or Flip Vertical (up from the top). It follows its floor and goes with it. Saved as an IfcSlab (ObjectType SLABEDGE). slabedge.create, slabedge.set, hostsweep.add_segments, hostsweep.remove_segments, hostsweep.flip

Layers

A floor or ceiling type can have layers like a wall type, listed from the top face down: a finish, a screed, the structure. Select a floor or ceiling and click Modify | Floors › Edit Assembly. The type's thickness becomes the layers' total, every floor of the type is drawn layer by layer in its materials, and in IFC the type has a material layer set that its floors use down from their top. Create Parts gives one part per layer. compound.set_layers, compound.get

Shape editing

A floor's top can be warped, for falls to a drain. Select the floor, then Modify | Floors › Add Point Add Point: click the point and type its height from the surface there (-50 for a drain). The top is triangulated through its corners and points into flat facets, and the slab follows it at its thickness. Reset Shape Reset Shape makes it flat again. Scripts can also raise corners and add split lines. shape.add_point, shape.add_split_line, shape.move, shape.set_elevation, shape.reset, shape.get

Not yet available: shape editing on roofs, variable layers (a shape-edited floor is drawn as one slab, without its layers), layered roof types, dragging points, and slab edge profiles other than rectangles.
