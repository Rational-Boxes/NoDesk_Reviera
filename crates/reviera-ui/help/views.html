Views and navigation

Plans, ceiling plans, sections, elevations and 3D views are saved in the model. The Project Browser opens them; each opens in its own tab. This page also covers moving around a view and the temporary ways of seeing inside a model: the section box, slices, and hiding and isolating elements.

See it in the Views and inspection sample (plans, sections, elevations, a scope box and 3D views with section boxes and slices), with its walkthrough.

Floor plans

A floor plan looks straight down at a level, cut at the plan's cut plane: cut walls and floors are drawn with poché, the rest below in projection, with door swings, room labels, grids and the markers of sections and elevations. Plans are drawn with each category's pens at their paper widths for the view's scale.

To create floor plans: View › Floor Plan, then tick the levels in the list. A new level gets its floor plan anyway. Floor Plan

Plans pan and zoom but do not orbit. With nothing selected, Properties show the plan's View Name, its Cut Plane (the height of the cut above the level, which you can edit) and its view range (Top, Bottom, View Depth). Curved walls, arched roofs and domes are cut at their true shape. view.create_plan, view.set_view_range

Ceiling plans

View › Ceiling Plan creates reflected ceiling plans of levels: cut at 2300 mm above the level, looking up, so they show the ceilings and what is above the cut. Ceiling Plan

Sections and elevations

To draw a section:

  1. Open a plan. View › Section.
  2. Click the start and the end of the section line. The view looks to the right of the line, from start to end.

Section

View › Building Elevations creates North, South, East and West elevations around the model. Building Elevations

Sections and elevations draw cut walls and floors with poché, everything up to the far clip in projection, level lines and grid bubbles. Plans show their section lines and elevation markers. In Properties, Far Clip Offset sets how far the view sees and Flipped turns it to look the other way. view.create_section, view.create_elevation, section.edit

Crop regions

A plan, section or elevation can be cropped: in Properties › Extents, tick Crop View, and the view draws only what lies inside its crop region, which first fits the model. Crop Region Visible draws the rectangle (and prints it). Tags of elements outside the crop are hidden. Annotation Crop adds a margin round it (5 mm on paper): dimensions and notes crossing it are cut, and those outside it hidden. Reset Crop fits the region to the model again. A plan assigned to a scope box is cropped to the box, and follows it when the box moves. Sheets show a cropped view at its cropped size. view.set_crop, view.reset_crop, view.set_annotation_crop

3D views

View › Default 3D View opens the model's default 3D view; New 3D View opens another, with its own camera. Each 3D view keeps its camera, Home, section box and slices in the saved file. Default 3D View

Perspective / Isometric (PI) switches between perspective and parallel projection.

Navigating

Orbit (3D)Left drag on empty space or the model. The view turns about the selection's centre, or about the point of the model under the cursor. Shift + middle drag also orbits.
PanMiddle or right drag. In plans, a left drag on nothing draws a selection box instead.
ZoomThe mouse wheel, about the cursor.
Zoom to Fit ZFShows the whole model, or the selection.
Previous / Next Pan/Zoom ZP ZNSteps back and forward through the views you had.
Home Alt+HomeReturns to the view's home orientation; View › Set as Home makes the current one its home.
Standard directionsView › Orient (Top, Bottom, Front, Back, Left, Right, the four isometric corners and the four diagonals), or the Orientation Cube.

Navigating changes only the camera: it adds nothing to the undo history and does not mark the model as changed.

The Orientation Cube

Every 3D view shows an Orientation Cube in its corner. Click a face, edge or corner (26 regions) to look from that side; the view turns there smoothly. The compass letters around it turn the view to that direction at its current height. The home button returns to the Home view, and a button switches perspective and isometric. Right-click the cube for Go Home, Set Current View as Home, the projection switch, and the standard directions.

Walking through a model

View › Walk (WK) walks through a 3D view at eye height.

W A S D or the arrow keysWalk forward, left, back and right
E or PgUp, Q or PgDnRise, sink
ShiftMove three times as fast
Left dragLook around
Mouse wheelChange the walking speed
EscStop walking

Section box and slices

These cut a 3D view open, to see inside. Cut solids are drawn capped. They are saved with the view and match BCF clipping planes.

View › Section Box SXTurns the view's section box on or off. Drag its orange face grips to resize it. Properties › Extents › Section Box also turns it on and off. Section Box
Selection Box BXFits the section box around the selected elements.
Horizontal SliceClick a point: the model above its height is cut away.
Vertical SliceClick a point: the model between you and it is cut away.
Clear SlicesRemoves the view's slice planes.

A view holds up to eight slice planes, each with a grip to drag it. view.set_section_box, view.fit_section_box, view.set_slice_planes

Temporary hide and isolate

These change only what the active view shows for now. They are never saved or printed. Select elements first, then:

Hide Element HHHides the selection.
Hide with ChildrenAlso hides what the selection contains or hosts (a wall's doors and windows).
Hide Category HC, Hide TypeHides every element of the selection's categories, or of its types.
Isolate Element HIShows only the selection (also with Children, Isolate Category IC, Isolate Type).
Isolate, Ghost Others HGShows the selection, and everything else ghosted; clicks pass through the ghosted elements.
Translucent HTDraws the selection translucent (also with Children). A translucent element can still be clicked and selected; hide it to click what is behind it.
Reset Hide/Isolate HRShows everything again. A middle click on empty space offers it too, since hidden elements cannot be clicked.

The tools are on View › Hide/Isolate and in the View menu; the quick modify menu and the Model Tree offer them too. view.temp_hide, view.temp_isolate, view.temp_translucent, view.temp_reset

Annotation in 3D

A 3D view shows the text notes, tags, dimensions and spots of the plans, sections and elevations as floating labels at their places in the model, drawn flat to the screen like tooltips so they read from any angle: a note above its level, a door's or window's tag at the insert, a room's tag over the room, a dimension's values along its line. View › Annotation › Annotation in 3D turns them on and off for the view; the button shows whether they are on. Annotation Visibility… has a check box for each class (text notes, dimensions, door tags, window tags, room tags and the other tagged categories) to show or hide each on its own, and under From one for each kind of view the annotation is drawn in (plans, sections, elevations); a new view shows all but dimensions, from the plans. A section's notes and dimensions stand on its cut; an elevation's are moved back along its view onto the facade they describe (or stay on its line when nothing is behind them); a tagged element is labelled once. The choice is kept with the view, and is one step to undo. Labels that would cover nearer ones move up or are left out, and so are labels whose place the model hides (seen through glass counts as seen); clear Hide labels behind the model to show them all. Click a label to select its annotation (its label is then edged in blue, and its properties show); double-click it to open the plan, section or elevation it is drawn in, zoomed to the annotation and with it selected. Zoom to Fit (ZF) with annotation selected frames it, in a plan or a 3D view. view.annotations_3d, view.set_3d_annotations

BCF viewpoints

A 3D view can be saved as a BCF viewpoint (.bcfv), the format coordination tools use for issues: View › Coordination › Export Viewpoint… writes the camera, the section box's faces and the slice planes as clipping planes, the hidden or isolated elements and the selection, by IFC GlobalId. Show Viewpoint… opens one (BCF 2.1 or 3.0) in the 3D view: six planes that form a box become its section box, the others slices; the components are hidden or isolated for now and selected. Elements the model does not have are counted. The camera and cut are one step to undo. bcf.export_viewpoint, bcf.import_viewpoint, view.apply_viewpoint; view.get reads a view's settings.

Managing views

In the Project Browser, double-click a view to open it, or right-click it to Open, Rename, Duplicate or Delete it. view.list, view.open, view.rename, view.duplicate, view.delete

Schedules and sheets are views too: see Documentation.
