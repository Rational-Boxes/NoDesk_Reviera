Sketch mode

Floors, ceilings, decks and roofs are made from a sketch: closed loops of lines and curves drawn in a plan. Sketch mode has its own contextual tab, and Finish turns the sketch into the element.

The sketch tab

While sketching, the ribbon shows Modify | Create Floor Boundary (or Create Ceiling Boundary, Create Deck Outline, Create Roof Footprint, or Edit Boundary and Edit Footprint when editing):

ToolWhat it does
Pick Walls (the default)Click a wall: its face on the cursor's side becomes a boundary line, locked so the element follows the wall when it moves. Tab takes the whole chain of walls joined or meeting end to end on that side. A curved wall gives its face's curve.
LineDraws chained lines, snapping like the drawing tools. Clicking the chain's first point closes it.
RectangleTwo opposite corners.
ArcIts start, its end, then a point on it (floors, ceilings and decks).
CircleIts centre, then a point on it (floors, ceilings and decks).
ModifySelects sketch curves to edit (see below).
Finish Edit ModeChecks the sketch and makes the element.
Cancel Edit ModeLeaves the sketch without changing the model.

Roof footprints also have Defines Slope and Overhang…: see Roofs.

Editing curves in the sketch

  • With Modify, click a curve to select it. Drag it to move it across (its neighbours stretch to meet it), or drag a corner to move the corner.
  • Properties show the selected curve's length, which you can edit (and for roofs, Defines Slope, Slope and Overhang).
  • Delete (or Backspace) removes the selected curve, or the last curve drawn when none is selected.
  • A curve moved off its wall drops its lock to the wall.

Finishing

Finish Edit Mode checks the sketch. Open ends, branches, crossings and overlapping curves are marked in red, and the sketch stays open to fix them. A loop inside another makes a hole. Cancel Edit Mode discards the sketch. Esc only drops a line or rectangle in progress: the sketch stays open until you finish or cancel it.

Changing a finished outline

In a plan, a selected floor, ceiling, deck or roof shows its outline with a grip at each corner and at the middle of each edge:

  • drag an edge grip to move that edge across (its neighbours stretch to meet it);
  • drag a corner grip to move the corner.

Each drag is one undoable edit, made without opening the sketch. To change the outline in sketch mode instead, use Edit Boundary (Modify | Floors, Modify | Ceilings) or Edit Footprint (Modify | Roofs). sketch.move_curve, sketch.move_corner
