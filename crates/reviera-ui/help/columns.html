Columns

Architectural columns are non-structural: casings, pilasters and decorative columns. Each has a type that gives its section, rectangular or round.

Placing columns

  1. Open a floor plan and click Architecture › Column Column.
  2. Choose the type in Properties (or Duplicate Type… to make a new size).
  3. Move the cursor: the column's section follows it, snapping to grid intersections, wall lines and other snaps. Space turns it by 90°.
  4. Click to place it. It runs from the plan's level up to the next level (3 m when there is none). The tool repeats until Esc.

column.place_architectural

Properties

Base Constraint, Base OffsetThe level it stands on, and how far above it.
Top Constraint, Top OffsetThe level its top follows, or Unconnected with an Unconnected Height. A column whose top follows a level grows when the level is raised.
RotationDegrees, counter-clockwise.
TypeThe type selector; Section (Rectangular or Round), Width and Depth (a round column's Diameter) are the type's, so they change every column of the type.

column.set, type.set_param (section, width, depth, diameter)

Attaching the top

Select columns and click Modify | Columns › Attach Top. Each column's top attaches to the roof, floor or ceiling directly above it, and follows it when it changes. Under a sloped roof the top is cut by the roof's underside, so it meets the roof flush. Detach Top returns the column to its own top constraint, as does deleting the roof.

Scripts can choose where the top goes over the column's section: maximum (the highest point of the underside, cut by it; the default), midline, or minimum (the lowest point, flat), with an offset. column.attach, column.detach

Moving and copying

Move, Copy, Rotate and Mirror work on columns (rotating turns the column too). Columns are deleted with their base or top level.

In IFC

A column is an IfcColumn (COLUMN, with Pset_ColumnCommon.LoadBearing false) of an IfcColumnType, contained in its base level; its body is written as a faceted B-rep.

Not yet available: attaching bases, taking the material of a joined wall, wrapping wall finishes round columns, Moves With Grids, and structural columns.
