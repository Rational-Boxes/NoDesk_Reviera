Curtain walls

A curtain wall is a surface divided by grid lines into panels, with mullions along the grid lines and round the border: storefronts and glazed façades.

Drawing a curtain wall

  1. Open a floor plan and click Architecture › Curtain Wall Curtain Wall.
  2. Choose its type in Properties (the default is Storefront: vertical grid lines at most 1.5 m apart, a transom 2.4 m up, 50 × 150 mm mullions, glazed panels).
  3. Click the start and the end of its base line; it snaps like the Wall tool. It runs from the plan's level up to the next level (3 m when there is none).

Drawn along an existing wall, the curtain wall is embedded: it cuts its own opening through the wall. curtain.create

Grids, mullions and panels

The type sets how each direction's grid lines are laid out:

Fixed DistanceLines at the spacing, from the start (or the middle, or the end: the instance's Justification), with what is left over at the other end.
Fixed NumberThat many lines, evenly spaced.
Maximum SpacingEqual panels no wider than the spacing.
Minimum SpacingEqual panels no narrower than the spacing.
NoneNo lines.

The type also sets the mullions' width and depth (0 for none), whether there are border mullions, and the panels: Glazed, Solid or Empty, and their thickness. These are type values, so they change every curtain wall of the type; Duplicate Type… makes a new one. type.set_param (u_layout, u_spacing, u_number, v_…, mullion_width, mullion_depth, border_mullions, panel, panel_thickness)

Each curtain wall has its own base and top, and for each direction a Justification (Beginning, Center, End) and Offset for Fixed Distance lines. curtain.set

Edit Grid: lines, segments and panels

Select a curtain wall and click Modify | Curtain Walls › Edit Grid:

  • Lines lists every grid line: U lines are vertical (Position along the wall from its start), V lines horizontal (up from its base). Add Line places a line by hand (ticked Manual); manual lines can be moved by editing their position, or deleted. The type's lines stay where the type puts them.
  • Segments is a row of 1s and 0s, one per segment from the start or base: a 0 removes that segment, so the panels either side become one panel and its mullion goes.
  • Panels swaps cells: Column (along) and Row (up), from 0, and the panel: Glazed, Solid, Empty or Door, a curtain-wall door that fills the cell, stands on the base and is written to IFC as an IfcDoor in the curtain wall.

OK applies it all in one step. curtain.add_grid, curtain.set_grid_segments, curtain.move_grid, curtain.delete_grid, curtain.set_panel_type, curtain.set_layout

Attaching the top to roofs

Select a curtain wall and use Attach Top with a roof, as for a wall (wall.attach): its top then follows the roof's underside along its length, so a gable end fills with glass. Attach it to more roofs and it follows each (the end wall of a multispan greenhouse runs under several); stretches no roof covers keep the wall's own height. Panels and mullions are clipped to the roof line and the top mullion follows it. Under a glazed roof the top stops below the roof's glazing bars. Detach returns it to its own height.

Moving and copying

Move, Copy, Rotate and Mirror work on curtain walls. They are deleted with their levels.

In IFC

An IfcCurtainWall of an IfcCurtainWallType, aggregating its panels (IfcPlate, CURTAIN_PANEL) and mullions (IfcMember, MULLION).

Not yet available: arc curtain walls, placing grid lines by clicking in an elevation, wall-type and family panels, mullion profiles and corner mullions, Adjust for Mullion Size, curtain systems by face; curtain-wall doors are not in door schedules yet, and a curtain wall does not yet refuse doors or windows in the stretch of wall it replaces. For sloped glazing see Roofs.
