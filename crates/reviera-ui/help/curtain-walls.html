Curtain walls

A curtain wall is a surface divided by grid lines into panels, with mullions along the grid lines and round the border: storefronts and glazed façades.

Drawing a curtain wall

  1. Open a floor plan and click Architecture › Curtain Wall Curtain Wall.
  2. Choose its type in Properties (the default is Storefront: vertical grid lines at most 1.5 m apart, a transom 2.4 m up, 50 × 150 mm mullions, glazed panels).
  3. Click the start and the end of its base line; it snaps like the Wall tool. It runs from the plan's level up to the next level (3 m when there is none).

Drawn along an existing wall, the curtain wall is embedded: it cuts its own opening through the wall. curtain.create

Grids, mullions and panels

The type sets how each direction's grid lines are laid out:

Fixed DistanceLines at the spacing, from the start (or the middle, or the end: the instance's Justification), with what is left over at the other end.
Fixed NumberThat many lines, evenly spaced.
Maximum SpacingEqual panels no wider than the spacing.
Minimum SpacingEqual panels no narrower than the spacing.
NoneNo lines.

The type also sets the mullions' width and depth (0 for none), whether there are border mullions, and the panels: Glazed, Solid or Empty, and their thickness. These are type values, so they change every curtain wall of the type; Duplicate Type… makes a new one. type.set_param (u_layout, u_spacing, u_number, v_…, mullion_width, mullion_depth, border_mullions, panel, panel_thickness)

Each curtain wall has its own base and top, and for each direction a Justification (Beginning, Center, End) and Offset for Fixed Distance lines. curtain.set

Moving and copying

Move, Copy, Rotate and Mirror work on curtain walls. They are deleted with their levels.

In IFC

An IfcCurtainWall of an IfcCurtainWallType, aggregating its panels (IfcPlate, CURTAIN_PANEL) and mullions (IfcMember, MULLION).

Not yet available: arc curtain walls, adding or removing single grid lines and segments, curtain-wall doors and other panel types, mullion profiles and corner mullions, Adjust for Mullion Size, curtain systems by face and sloped glazing; a curtain wall does not yet refuse doors or windows in the stretch of wall it replaces.
