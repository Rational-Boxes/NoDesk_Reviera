Site: toposolids and property lines

A toposolid is solid ground: a triangulated top surface with upright sides and a flat bottom. It can be sub-divided into roads and paths, graded with its cut and fill reported, and simplified, and trees and other objects stand on it.

Making a toposolid

Open the plan of the level the site is on and click Massing & Site › Toposolid Toposolid:

  • From a boundary: draw a closed loop of model lines, select it first, and type the ground's height. Points added later shape it.
  • From a points file: with nothing selected, pick a CSV, TXT or XYZ file with one point per row (x y z in metres, separated by spaces, tabs or commas; a header row is skipped). The boundary is the points' outline.

Plans draw contours on toposolids, heavier every fifth, at the interval in Site Settings (default 1 m through elevation 0). Toposolids are cut like other elements where they rise above the plan's cut plane. toposolid.create, toposolid.set_points (add, move, remove or lock points), toposolid.info

Sub-divisions and excavation

Select a toposolid and a closed loop of model lines on it, click Sub-Divide, and type its height above the surface. A positive height raises a layer on the ground (a path, a planting bed); a negative one excavates it, as for a road: the ground below loses that volume, reported as the excavation volume in toposolid.info. Sub-divisions follow the ground when its points change. toposolid.subdivide

Graded region, cut and fill

  1. Select the existing ground and click Graded Region. Choose to copy every point, or only the boundary's.
  2. The existing toposolid is hidden and kept; change the copy's points.
  3. Select the copy and click Cut/Fill: the volumes of earth removed and added against the existing ground, and the net.

toposolid.create_graded_region, toposolid.cut_fill

Simplify surface

A dense survey makes a slow model. Select the toposolid, click Simplify Surface and type how many points to keep. The points that change the surface least go first; boundary and locked points always stay, and the status bar says how far the new surface is from the old points at most. toposolid.simplify takes a point count or a maximum vertical error.

Property lines

Click Property Line. With a closed loop of model lines selected it follows them; otherwise type a table, one side per row: its distance and its bearing, such as 30000 N 45°30'00" E (from north or south toward east or west) or an azimuth in degrees clockwise from north. A closed line reports its area. Property lines are drawn dash-dotted in red. propertyline.create (also arcs: radius and side), propertyline.area

Site components

Import a tree, car or bench (Architecture › Component › Import Object), then click Site Component, pick it and click in a plan. On a toposolid it stands on the surface and follows it when the ground changes. sitecomponent.place
