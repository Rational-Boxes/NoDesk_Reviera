Railings and decks

Railings are sketched along a path, put along the open edges of floors, decks and flat roofs, or follow stairs. Gates and openings are added where you click. Decks lay out their boards and framing one by one, with a cut list.

To sketch a railing

  1. Open a plan. Architecture › Circulation › Railing. Railing
  2. Click the path's points.
  3. Click the first point again to close the path, or press Esc to finish an open path.

A railing sketched over a deck, floor or flat roof stands on its top. Each railing has a top and a bottom rail, posts at its ends and corners, and balusters spaced by its type. railing.create

Guarding edges

Select floors, decks or flat roofs, then Modify | Floors › Guard Edges (or Modify | Roofs › Guard Edges): railings go along every open edge, leaving out edges with a wall along them. They follow the host's outline when it changes. Guard Edges railing.create_on_edges

Stair railings

A new stair gets railings on both sides. They follow the nosing line, run flat across landings and round the outside of a turn, and extend when the stair does. To add them to a selected stair: Modify | Stairs › Railings. railing.create_on_stair

Openings and gates

  1. Select the railing.
  2. Modify | Railings › Opening, Gate or Double Gate.
  3. Click on the railing where each goes, in a plan or on the railing in 3D. Esc ends.

An opening gets a post on each side. A gate fills its opening with one leaf, a double gate with two, swinging in or out. A gate is its own door element (IfcDoor, GATE); deleting it leaves its opening. The House sample's front railing has a gate, a double gate and an opening. railing.add_opening, railing.add_gate, railing.move_feature, railing.remove_feature, railing.features

Decks

  1. Open a plan. Architecture › Circulation › Deck. Deck
  2. Sketch the outline (Create Deck Outline: Line, Rectangle, Arc, Circle or Pick Walls) and finish. See Sketch mode.

The deck's boards are laid out one by one: courses across from the start edge (the last one ripped, or the courses balanced), every end joint on a joist and staggered, picture-frame borders mitred at the corners, and each piece cut from the shortest stock length that fits, with offcuts reused. The framing is laid out too: joists, beams, posts and footings.

Select the deck to change, in Properties:

BoardsBoard Width, Board Thickness, Gap, Direction, Stock Lengths, Picture Frame Rows, Joint Stagger, Balance Courses.
FramingJoist Spacing, Beam Spacing, Post Spacing.

A deck is a floor, so its outline grips, Edit Boundary and Guard Edges work on it. deck.create, deck.set_boards, deck.set_framing, deck.boards

Cut list

The selected deck's Properties end with its Cut List: the boards, the stock lengths to buy, the framing (joists, beams, posts and footings) and the screws. deck.cut_list returns it to scripts.

Not yet available: continuous handrails, baluster families, deck ledgers, span tables, breaker boards and per-board overrides.
