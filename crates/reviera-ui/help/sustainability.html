Energy, daylight and carbon

The Sustainability tab studies the building's energy, daylight and carbon from the model you already have: the MEP spaces become the energy model, the weather file gives the climate, and the heavy calculations run as background jobs while you keep working. Results are kept with the project and say when the model has changed since.

Try it on the Energy house (loads, annual energy, carbon, exports) and the Daylight studios (daylight factor, solar). Each has a walkthrough of how it was built.

Site and weather

Set the project's latitude, longitude, elevation and time zone with Location, or find them by name with Find Place and fetch the ground height with Site Elevation. Import an EPW weather file with Weather: it is copied beside the saved project, and its design temperatures are worked out from its hours. Without one, PVGIS Weather fetches a typical year for the site.

The energy model

Every enclosed MEP space (Analyze › Spaces & Zones) is an analytical space; its walls, windows, doors, floor and roof are heat-transfer surfaces, interior where another space lies beyond. Energy Model lists them with their areas and heat loss; its Thermal Values… sets a type's U-value and a window's solar heat gain. The space types (Manage › MEP Settings › Building/Space Types) give the loads, people, outdoor air, set points and opening hours.

Heating and cooling loads

Heating & Cooling Loads works out each space's and zone's design loads from the weather's design conditions, and writes the peaks to the spaces; Loads Report shows them by component.

Annual energy

Annual Energy runs a year of hours through the weather file: heating and cooling loads, delivered energy by end use, energy use intensity and operational carbon from your grid factor. The Engine is Reviera's own hourly model, or EnergyPlus where it is installed. Energy Results lists the months, the year and each space. For Great Britain, Grid Carbon (GB) gives a grid factor.

Daylight factor

Daylight Factor puts a grid of sensors in every space and works out how much of the overcast sky reaches each through the windows, plus the light reflected in the room. Daylight Results gives each space's average, minimum and uniformity, and Show on Model colours the sensors in 3D.

Solar studies

Solar Study works out the sun on the building's faces over a period, with shading, and its PV potential; PVGIS Check compares a PV system's yield with PVGIS's.

Carbon

Embodied Carbon totals the materials' carbon from the model's layers and quantities; annual energy adds the operational carbon.

gbXML and HBJSON

File › Export gbXML… writes the energy model for other energy tools, and Import gbXML Loads… reads loads they worked out back onto the spaces. File › Export HBJSON… writes it for Ladybug Tools.

Online data

PVGIS, place search, elevation and grid carbon are online services Reviera calls only when you ask, and each can be switched off in Data Connectors. Help › Data Sources shows the credit results made with their data must carry.
