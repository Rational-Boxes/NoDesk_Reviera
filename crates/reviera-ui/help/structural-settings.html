Regional standards

The model's building code (Model Properties) sets its region: which section catalogues come first, the default steel grade and concrete class (ASTM A992 and 4000 psi in the US; S355 and C30/37 in Europe; 350W and 30 MPa in Canada; 300PLUS and 32 MPa in Australia), the timber grades, and the load combination template (ASCE 7 or EN 1990, see Loads).

Materials

The materials library holds steels, concretes, sawn timber and glulam with their strengths and moduli (E, G, density), used by the analysis for stiffness and self-weight. A section type's structural material is a Properties row; a wall's or floor's is its type's structure layer.
