MEP connectors and systems

Mechanical, plumbing and electrical components carry connectors: the points where ducts, pipes and cables join them. Connected components form networks, and terminals, fixtures and equipment are grouped into systems such as Supply Air 1 or Domestic Cold Water 1. The tools are on the Systems tab.

Components with connectors

Load MEP Types adds Reviera's starter types: Air Handling Unit, Supply Diffuser, Return Grille, Exhaust Fan, Water Heater, Circulation Pump, Lavatory, Water Closet, Distribution Board, Receptacle and Ceiling Luminaire. They are simple boxes with typical connectors, for laying out systems before you have manufacturers' content; their sizes and flows are placeholders to change. MEP Component places any type that has connectors, like Place Component.

A connector has a domain (Duct, Pipe, Electrical, Cable Tray or Conduit), a point and an outward direction, a shape and size, and for ducts and pipes a system classification (Supply Air, Return Air, Exhaust Air, Domestic Cold Water, Sanitary, Hydronic Supply, …), a flow direction (In, Out or Bidirectional) and a flow. Electrical connectors carry the voltage, poles, load and power factor. Scripts and agents add connectors to any component type with connector.add; a placed copy can have its own flow (connector.set).

When elements with connectors are selected, each connector is drawn as a ring with a stalk pointing out: blue for ducts, green for pipes, orange for electrical, and red while it is open.

Ducts and pipes

Duct (DT) and Pipe (PL) ask for the type, shape and size, the middle elevation (the centreline's height above the level), the system type and, for pipes, a slope. Then click the run's points in a plan:

  • an elbow goes at every corner;
  • press E to change the elevation: the run rises or drops at the last point, with an elbow at each end of the riser;
  • end (or start) the run on the side of another duct or pipe, square to it, and a tee or a tap joins them;
  • Enter or Esc makes the run; the tool stays for the next one.

A run that meets a connector connects to it, and joins that connector's system when it carries the same system type. Pipes drawn with a slope fall along their level legs from the first point. Cap Open Ends closes the selection's open ends.

Which elbow, tee, tap or cap is used comes from the type's routing preferences: for each role a list of parts with a size range, tried from the top. Where none fits, nothing is inserted, the end is left open and a warning says so. routing_preferences.set changes them and mep_run.reapply_type refits existing runs. The fittings are Reviera's own compact mitred parts; their proportions, like the starter types' sizes and flows, are provisional values awaiting review by an engineer.

Connections

Placing or moving a component connects its open connectors that meet another's: in the same place, facing each other, of the same domain, shape and size. Connect does it for the selection. mep.connect and mep.disconnect join and separate two connectors by hand; a duct connector never joins a pipe connector, and different sizes are refused until fittings with transitions exist.

Systems

Select terminals, fixtures or equipment and choose Create System. Pick the system type (the list offers the classifications of the selection's connectors) and a name, or leave the name empty for the next free one. Each element joins by its connectors of that classification; a supply diffuser cannot join a return air system, and a connector is in one system at a time.

  • Select Equipment: select the air handling unit, pump or water heater that serves a system, then choose the system.
  • Add To System and Remove From System change the members.
  • Divide System splits a system whose members form separate networks into Name 1, Name 2, and so on.
  • Disconnect Equipment stops the equipment serving the system.

System types

Every classification has a built-in type of its name with an abbreviation (SA, RA, DCW, …) and a colour. Members of a system are drawn in their type's colour. System Types renames a type, changes its abbreviation and colour, or makes a duplicate, for example a separate Supply Air type for laboratories.

System Browser

System Browser (F9) lists every system by discipline and classification, with its equipment and members, and the connectors that are in no system under Unassigned. Click a node to select its elements. It follows every change to the model.

Checking systems

Check Duct Systems and Check Pipe Systems mark problems in the view with a "!" triangle; the status bar gives the count and the first one:

  • a system not well connected: it has no equipment, or some members are not connected to the equipment;
  • a flow mismatch: the equipment's preset flow differs from its terminals' total, or a calculated outlet has nothing connected;
  • a flow direction mismatch: two connected connectors both flowing in, or both out.

Show Disconnects asks which domains to check and marks every open connector with a red ring until it is connected. Press each button again to turn its markers off. The checks never change the model. mep_check.systems, mep_check.show_disconnects
