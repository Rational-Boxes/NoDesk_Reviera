Walls

Walls are drawn on a level as chains of straight segments, arcs or splines. They join at corners by themselves, carry doors and windows, bound rooms, and can attach their tops to a roof.

Drawing walls

  1. Open the plan of the level the walls stand on (the tool uses the plan's level; in a 3D view, the lowest level).
  2. Architecture › Build › Wall (WA). Wall
  3. Optionally choose the wall type in Properties (nothing is selected while the tool runs).
  4. Click the start point, then each next point. Each click ends one wall and starts the next, so a chain of walls follows the clicks.
  5. Instead of clicking the next point, type a length and press Enter: the wall runs that far toward the cursor. Any unit can be typed (4500, 4.5 m, 12' 6", 8 6 for 8'-6" in feet and inches), and arithmetic (1200+600, (2*900)/2); Backspace edits it. The Grid, Ref Plane, Model Line and Scope Box tools take typed lengths the same way.
  6. Esc ends the chain; Esc again ends the tool.

Snapping. Points snap to wall ends, wall midpoints, points on walls' location lines, cut edges of other elements in the plan, and the nearest 15° direction from the previous point.

Exterior side. The status bar says which side of the line the exterior face is on (the left, as you draw). Draw a building's outline clockwise and the exteriors face out. Spacebar flips the side while drawing.

Height. A new wall rises to the level above, when there is one; otherwise it gets an unconnected height of 3000 mm. Change either afterwards in Properties.

The House sample's guide starts with a rectangle of walls: click (0, 0), type 10000 along x and Enter, then 7000 up, 10000 back and down to the start. wall.create

Arc walls

  1. Architecture › Build › Arc Wall. Arc Wall
  2. Click the wall's start, then its end, then a point the arc passes through.

A full circle takes two half arcs, as the round tower of the Manor and the drum of the Observatory.

Spline walls

  1. Architecture › Build › Spline Wall. Spline Wall
  2. Click the points the wall passes through; Enter ends it at the last point.

Curved walls (arc and spline) carry doors and windows along the curve, square to it, bound rooms, attach to roofs and join straight walls with mitred corners.

Properties

Select a wall to see its rows in Properties:

Base Constraint, Base OffsetThe level the wall stands on, and how far above it its base is.
Top ConstraintA level the top reaches, or Unconnected.
Unconnected HeightThe height when the top is unconnected (it follows the level otherwise).
Top OffsetHow far above (or below) the top level the top is.
TypeChoose a type, or Duplicate Type…. The type's Width changes every wall of that type. See Types.

To flip a wall's exterior and interior sides afterwards, select it and press Spacebar, or Modify | Walls › Flip Wall. wall.set_constraints, wall.flip, wall.set_location

Joins

Walls that meet join by themselves: mitred at L corners, and joined at T junctions. Joins are recomputed as walls move. A join can be disallowed at either end of a wall with the wall.set_join_allowed command; wall.joins_query lists a wall's joins.

Attaching to roofs

A wall's top can attach to a roof or dome above it: gable walls rise to the ridge, and a drum rises to its dome. The part of the wall above the roof's lowest point follows the roof.

  1. Select the walls. Modify | Walls › Attach Top/Base.
  2. Click the roof.

Detach Top/Base returns the selected walls to their own tops. To attach every wall under a roof at once, select the roof and use Modify | Roofs › Attach Walls; see Roofs. wall.attach, wall.detach

Layers (Edit Assembly)

A wall type can have layers: finishes, insulation, structure and so on, exterior first. Select a wall and click Modify | Walls › Edit Assembly; the dialog lists the layers of its type:

FunctionStructure, Substrate, Thermal/Air, Finish 1, Finish 2 or Membrane (a membrane has no thickness).
Priority1 (the strongest) to 9; each function has a default. Walking out from the core, priorities must not get stronger.
MaterialThe material's name (written to IFC).
ThicknessIn the model's units; at least 3.2 mm except membranes.
Wraps, CoreTick Core on the core's layers, which must be next to each other.

Insert, Delete, Up and Down edit the rows. OK checks the layers and applies them to the type, so every wall of the type changes; a problem is shown and the dialog stays open. The type's width is then the total of its layers (its Properties show the layers as Structure), and plans draw the boundaries between the layers inside cut walls. compound.get, compound.set_layers

In IFC the type has an IfcMaterialLayerSet and its walls an IfcMaterialLayerSetUsage.

Editing walls

  • A selected wall shows a grip at each end: drag it to move that end.
  • Drag selected walls to move them. Walls carry their doors and windows when they are moved, copied or mirrored.
  • Offset, Trim/Extend, Split and Align are on the Modify tab: see Modify tools.

Walls are saved as IfcWall with an axis and a body (curved and attached walls as faceted B-reps), typed, contained in their storey and connected to their neighbours.

Not yet available: location lines other than the centreline, layer cleanup at joins and wrapping at ends and openings, layered floors and roofs, a material library, T joins into the side of a curved wall, and attaching wall bases or attaching to floors and ceilings.
