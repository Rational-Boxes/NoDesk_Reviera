Foundations

Footings, wall foundations, foundation slabs, piles and pile caps, on Structure › Foundation. Their types default to the region's concrete and sizes (for example 1800 × 1800 × 500 mm pads, or 6'-0" × 6'-0" × 1'-6" in US models).

Isolated footings

  • Isolated (F T): click in a plan; the footing's top is at the level.
  • At Columns: one under each selected column, centred, turned with it, its top at the column's base, following the column when it moves; columns that have one are skipped. Scripts can also place them at grid crossings (footing.place_at_grids).

Wall foundations

Select structural walls and click Wall (F W): a strip footing under each, bearing (a width centred on the wall) or retaining (a toe beyond the exterior face and a heel beyond the interior one), with an end extension. It follows its wall and goes with it.

Foundation slabs

Slab: sketch the boundary; the slab's top is at the level and its thickness below it (a slab on grade or a raft).

Piles and pile caps

Pile places single piles, bored or driven, their top at the level. Pile Cap places a cap with its piles laid out under it (rows, columns and spacing from the cap's type; four piles by default), which follow it and go with it.

Information and IFC

foundation.info reports each foundation's top, Elevation at Bottom, area, volume and host. Footings are IfcFooting (PAD_FOOTING, STRIP_FOOTING, PILE_CAP aggregating its IfcPiles); foundation slabs IfcSlab (BASESLAB).

Not yet available: editing pile layouts in Properties, joins between slabs and footings, picking walls for a slab's boundary, foundation tags.
