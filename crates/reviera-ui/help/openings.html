Vertical openings

A vertical opening cuts a floor or ceiling plumb, for example over a stair. In the window, openings come from the Stair tool's headroom offer; they are then reshaped with their grips.

Making an opening

When a stair passes under a floor or ceiling that leaves it short of the building code's headroom, Reviera offers to open it over the stair's full width. Accept the offer when the stair is made, or later select the stair and use Modify | Stairs › Stair Openings. See Stairs.

Scripts and agents can make openings of any outline with opening.create_vertical.

In plans

Plans of the host's level draw each opening as its outline crossed corner to corner. Click the outline or the cross to select it.

Reshaping an opening

  1. Select the opening in a plan.
  2. Drag a corner grip to move the corner, or an edge grip to move that edge across.

Each drag is one undo step. An opening partly outside its floor notches the floor's edge. The opening moves with its floor or ceiling, and is deleted with it. opening.set_loops

In IFC the slab stays whole and an IfcOpeningElement voids it.

Not yet available: a sketch tool for openings in the window, openings in roofs, shafts through several floors, and guards placed round an opening by themselves (a railing can be sketched along its edge).
