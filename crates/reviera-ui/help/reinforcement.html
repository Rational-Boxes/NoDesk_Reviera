Reinforcement

Rebar sets in concrete columns, beams, floors and walls, and area, path and fabric reinforcement in floors and walls, on the Concrete tab. A set is one element: a shape, a bar type and a layout, worked out from its host inside the host's cover. When the host changes, its bars follow.

What can hold rebar

  • Structural columns of concrete or precast concrete, and concrete beams.
  • Structural floors, and bearing, shear or combined structural walls, whose type has a concrete layer. A generic type's structure layer with no material named counts as concrete.

If a host stops qualifying (it is made non-structural, or its material changes), its rebar is kept and shown as orphaned in Properties, with the reason. Move it to another host with rebar.rehost, or delete it. Rebar is deleted with its host, and the delete confirmation counts it under Structural Rebar.

Placing rebar

Select one or more hosts and click Rebar. The dialog offers the placements the selected kind of host takes:

  • Links in the section (columns and beams): closed links (shape 51) with 135° hooks into the core, spread along the member.
  • Bars at a face (columns and beams): straight, L or U bars (shapes 00, 11, 21) running along the member at its top, bottom, left or right face, spread across it. The legs of L and U bars turn into the section.
  • Floor layers: straight bars in the bottom or top layer, in a plan direction (0° is along x). Each bar is clipped to the floor's outline and openings, less the edge cover.
  • Wall faces: straight bars at the exterior or interior face, vertical or horizontal.

Bars keep inside each face's cover. If a shape cannot fit, it is refused and the message names the width, depth or length that is too small.

The bars run between the covers, so the outer bars' surfaces sit at the cover. A selected set's Properties show its shape, bar type, quantity, bar length, total length, mass and shape dimensions. You can change its Rule, Number, Spacing and Formula there.

Cover

Each host face (top, bottom, exterior, interior, other) uses a named cover type. The defaults depend on the kind of host: columns and beams use Beams and columns (35 mm, or 1 1/2" in US models), floors use Interior, and walls use Exterior outside. Select hosts and click Cover to give one face, or all faces, a cover type. Their rebar moves to suit. A cover type in use can be deleted only after choosing a replacement (rebar_cover_type.delete).

Reinforcement Settings

Reinforcement Settings holds:

  • Rounding of bar lengths: nearest, up or down, to an increment of at most 100 mm. Rounded lengths are shown as exact [rounded], for example 2437 [2440].
  • Hooks in shape definitions, which is decided before the first rebar is placed.
  • The project's cover types, one per line as name = mm. Change an offset, or add a line to make a new type.

Bars, hooks and shapes

Bar types come from the region's catalogue: ASTM A615 #3 to #18 in US models, or 6 to 50 mm B500B bars elsewhere. Bend diameters follow ACI 318 or EN 1992-1-1. Shapes come from an open BS 8666 shape-code catalogue: 00 straight, 11 L, 21 U and 51 closed link. The default bend and hook values are a starting point, so check them against the governing code. Scripts can add project bar and hook types (rebar_bar_type.create, rebar_hook_type.create).

In IFC

Each set is an IfcReinforcingBar (MAIN, or LIGATURE for links) with a body for every bar, its diameter, area and length. It is typed by an IfcReinforcingBarType that carries the shape code as BendingShapeCode and the A, B, C… dimensions as BendingParameters. Each host aggregates its sets.

Area reinforcement

Select a structural floor or wall, click Area and set the layers. Then lay it over the whole host, or sketch the boundary on the host: on a floor's top in a plan or 3D view, on a wall's face in a 3D view. Click its corners; they snap to the host's corners and edges. A click on the first corner (or Enter) closes it, and R draws it as a rectangle by two corners. The major direction edge is drawn doubled; M moves it to the next edge. The major bars run along it and the minor ones square to it. XY opens Coordinates to type the corners or correct the sketched ones.

  • Up to four layers: top and bottom (exterior and interior for walls), each major and minor. Each face's minor layer sits inside its major one.
  • One bar type and a maximum spacing or a number of bars, with optional hooks turned into the host. Additional offsets move a face's layers further in, to stack systems.
  • The boundary and the host's openings cut the bars, less the edge cover.

The system's Properties list its layers and edit their spacing. With a system selected, Coordinates edits its boundary or path. Remove Area System keeps the layers as ordinary rebar sets. It needs area and path reinforcement as real rebar on (reinforcement_settings.set area_path_rebar), which is decided before the first area or path system.

Path reinforcement

Path lays bars square to an open path that you sketch along the host, usually on a slab's edge (Enter finishes). Ticks show the side the bars go; F flips it. The bars are spaced along the path between its end covers. The primary bars have a type, a length and optional legs turned into the host at both ends. Alternating bars take every second place, with their own type, length and an offset from the path. Properties show the counts, length, mass and steel volume, and change the side and spacing.

Fabric

Sheet types come from the region's catalogue (BS 4483 A142 … A393, B385 and B503 in 4.8 × 2.4 m sheets; WRI 6x6 W1.4 … W4.0 styles in 8 × 20 ft sheets) or the project's own (fabric_sheet_type.create, fabric_wire_type.create). Check them against the supplier.

  • Fabric Area lays sheets over a sketched boundary or the whole host from its major edge, the sheets' length along it. They overlap by the end and side laps; leave them blank for a crossing spacing plus 50 mm. Staggered starts every second row half a sheet back. Each sheet is cut by the boundary, the openings and the cover, and Properties count the cut sheets.
  • Fabric Sheet places one sheet: click its first corner, then a point along its length (Enter keeps the plane's x direction). The sheet's outline follows the cursor. It is cut by the host's cover when asked.

In IFC, area, path and fabric systems are IfcElementAssembly (REINFORCEMENT_UNIT) elements. Their bars (IfcReinforcingBar) and sheets (IfcReinforcingMesh, typed by IfcReinforcingMeshType with sizes and spacings) are written under them each time the model is saved. Rebar inside its concrete is drawn but hidden by it. Hide the host (HH) to see the bars.

Rebar numbers and partitions

Every rebar set and fabric sheet has a Rebar Number, shown in Properties. Identical ones share it within their partition: same bar type, shape and segment values after rounding, or the same sheet type and cut. Partition is free text (a level, phase, top or bottom…), edited in Properties; a fabric area's sheets take the area's. Rebar and fabric number separately. A new identity takes the next free number and keeps it, so numbers do not move when other bars go. Reinforcement Numbers (Concrete › Settings) shows a partition's sequence:

  • Change a number in the grid. A number another row has is refused, and that row is marked.
  • Tick Locked to keep a number through renumbering.
  • Start number, Remove gaps (1, 2, 4, 7 becomes 1, 2, 3, 4) and Renumber in creation order skip locked numbers.
  • Select Elements selects the elements of the row under the cursor.

The number is written as the bar's or sheet's IFC Tag.

Rebar in views

Each view decides how it draws each set or system:

  • Unobscured: the bars show through their concrete. Otherwise the concrete hides them, as any solid would.
  • Solid (3D views only): tubes at Fine detail. Everywhere else bars are drawn as centrelines.
  • Presentation (sets): Show All, First/Last, Middle, or Select Bars, where you click the bars to show and press Enter. First/Last and Middle also draw the set's extent line with end ticks.

Set them from the contextual tab of a selected set, or for several views at once in View Visibility States. Presentation only changes what is drawn: quantities, numbers and the IFC export keep every bar. Clicking a drawn bar selects its set. The presentation of sets with none of their own comes from Reinforcement Settings.
