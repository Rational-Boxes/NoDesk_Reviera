Reinforcement

Rebar sets in concrete columns, beams, floors and walls, on the Concrete tab. A set is one element: a shape, a bar type and a layout, worked out from its host inside the host's cover. When the host changes, its bars follow.

What can hold rebar

  • Structural columns of concrete or precast concrete, and concrete beams.
  • Structural floors, and bearing, shear or combined structural walls, whose type has a concrete layer. A generic type's structure layer with no material named counts as concrete.

If a host stops qualifying (it is made non-structural, or its material changes), its rebar is kept and shown as orphaned in Properties, with the reason. Move it to another host with rebar.rehost, or delete it. Rebar is deleted with its host, and the delete confirmation counts it under Structural Rebar.

Placing rebar

Select one or more hosts and click Rebar. The dialog offers the placements the selected kind of host takes:

  • Links in the section (columns and beams): closed links (shape 51) with 135° hooks into the core, spread along the member.
  • Bars at a face (columns and beams): straight, L or U bars (shapes 00, 11, 21) running along the member at its top, bottom, left or right face, spread across it. The legs of L and U bars turn into the section.
  • Floor layers: straight bars in the bottom or top layer, in a plan direction (0° is along x). Each bar is clipped to the floor's outline and openings, less the edge cover.
  • Wall faces: straight bars at the exterior or interior face, vertical or horizontal.

Bars keep inside each face's cover. If a shape cannot fit, it is refused and the message names the width, depth or length that is too small.

The bars run between the covers, so the outer bars' surfaces sit at the cover. A selected set's Properties show its shape, bar type, quantity, bar length, total length, mass and shape dimensions. You can change its Rule, Number, Spacing and Formula there.

Cover

Each host face (top, bottom, exterior, interior, other) uses a named cover type. The defaults depend on the kind of host: columns and beams use Beams and columns (35 mm, or 1 1/2" in US models), floors use Interior, and walls use Exterior outside. Select hosts and click Cover to give one face, or all faces, a cover type. Their rebar moves to suit. A cover type in use can be deleted only after choosing a replacement (rebar_cover_type.delete).

Reinforcement Settings

Reinforcement Settings holds:

  • Rounding of bar lengths: nearest, up or down, to an increment of at most 100 mm. Rounded lengths are shown as exact [rounded], for example 2437 [2440].
  • Hooks in shape definitions, which is decided before the first rebar is placed.
  • The project's cover types, one per line as name = mm. Change an offset, or add a line to make a new type.

Bars, hooks and shapes

Bar types come from the region's catalogue: ASTM A615 #3 to #18 in US models, or 6 to 50 mm B500B bars elsewhere. Bend diameters follow ACI 318 or EN 1992-1-1. Shapes come from an open BS 8666 shape-code catalogue: 00 straight, 11 L, 21 U and 51 closed link. The default bend and hook values are a starting point, so check them against the governing code. Scripts can add project bar and hook types (rebar_bar_type.create, rebar_hook_type.create).

In IFC

Each set is an IfcReinforcingBar (MAIN, or LIGATURE for links) with a body for every bar, its diameter, area and length. It is typed by an IfcReinforcingBarType that carries the shape code as BendingShapeCode and the A, B, C… dimensions as BendingParameters. Each host aggregates its sets.
