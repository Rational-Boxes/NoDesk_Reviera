Ramps

A ramp is a sloped run between two heights, with landings where it turns and where a run would be longer than its type allows.

To draw a ramp

  1. Open a floor plan and click Architecture › Ramp Ramp.
  2. In Properties, set the Height it climbs (600 mm unless you change it) and its Width (1.2 m).
  3. Click the points of its centreline from the bottom up. The status bar shows the run the type's maximum slope needs for that height, and how much is drawn so far.
  4. Press Enter to make the ramp. Esc drops the last point, then ends the tool.

Each straight segment is a run; where two runs meet, a square landing as wide as the ramp turns the corner. A run longer than the type's Maximum Incline Length is split into equal flights with a landing between each two, and the landings lengthen the ramp along its path. The height is shared so every flight has the same slope.

A ramp steeper than its type's maximum slope is still made, with a warning: lengthen the path or lower the height.

Properties

Base Constraint, Base OffsetThe level its bottom is on, and how far above it.
Top Constraint, Top OffsetThe level its top reaches, or Unconnected with a height.
WidthAcross the ramp.
TypeMaximum Slope (1 in 12 by default), Maximum Incline Length (9 m), Thickness (150 mm) and Shape: Thick, a slab under the sloped surface, or Solid, filled down to the base. These change every ramp of the type.

ramp.set, type.set_param

Moving and copying

Move, Copy, Rotate and Mirror work on ramps. A ramp is deleted with its base or top level.

In IFC

A ramp is an IfcRamp of an IfcRampType, contained in its base level, with a faceted B-rep body.

Not yet available: curved ramps, editing the path with grips, railings that follow a ramp, and ramp flights and landings as separate IFC parts.
