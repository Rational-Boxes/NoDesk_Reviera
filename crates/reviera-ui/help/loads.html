Loads and supports

Load cases, their combinations, the loads in them and the supports the model stands on, on Analyze › Loads.

Load cases

Load Cases opens one dialog: the design code (ASCE 7 or EN 1990), load natures (dead, live, roof live, snow, wind … from the code, and your own), load cases (each with a nature, a category and self-weight), combinations and usage tags. Changes apply as you make them; Cancel undoes them all. Renaming a case renames it in the formulas; deleting it deletes its loads and takes it out of the combinations, after saying how many.

Combinations

A combination is a formula such as 1.2*DL + 1.6*LL + 0.5*SNOW over cases or other combinations, with a limit state and usages. Generate… (or Generate Combinations) proposes the code's combinations offline: ASCE/SEI 7-22 strength combinations, or EN 1990 expression 6.10 with each variable action leading in turn and the serviceability ones. Cases whose nature the code does not know are named.

Placing loads

Each tool asks for its case and values, then places on what you click in a plan at its level:

  • Point Load: at a node, or at the clicked point along a member (Fx, Fy, Fz in kN).
  • Line Load: along a member, uniform or varying (kN/m).
  • Area Load: on a horizontal panel (kPa). For analysis it is carried to the members lying on the panel across its span, each taking the strip halfway to its neighbours.

Values are in project axes (gravity is negative z) or a host's own axes; load.set changes a load's case and values.

Supports

Column Base Supports pins every column's foot; Point Support places pinned, fixed or roller supports at clicked nodes. Scripts can give each degree of freedom fixed, released or a spring.

load_case.*, load_combination.*, load.*, boundary_condition.*

Not yet available: loads varying across a panel or inside a drawn boundary, two-way slabs, line and area supports.
