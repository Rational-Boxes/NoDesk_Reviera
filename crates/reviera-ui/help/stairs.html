Stairs

Stairs are drawn run by run in a plan and complete themselves to the level above. They get railings on both sides, and offer to open the floors above them where the building code's headroom is short.

To draw a stair

  1. Open the plan of the stair's bottom level. Architecture › Circulation › Stair (ST). Stair
  2. Click the start of the first run, then toward its end. The run holds as many risers as its length allows at the tread depth; the status bar counts the risers left.
  3. To turn, click the start of the next run: an automatic landing joins the runs. Straight, L and U arrangements work this way.
  4. When every riser is placed the stair is made.

The number of risers is the fewest that keep each riser within the type's maximum height (180 mm by default). stair.create

The House sample has an L and a U stair; its guide gives the clicks.

Reaching the level above

  • A run drawn to within a tread of the length that places every riser left snaps to it: the status bar says it reaches the level above, and the click makes the stair.
  • Esc makes the stair from the runs drawn so far, the last run carrying on to the top. With no run drawn, Esc cancels.
  • The stair keeps reaching its top level when levels move, and its railings extend with it.

Headroom and stair openings

When a new stair passes under a floor or ceiling that leaves less headroom than the building code asks (measured from the line of the nosings), Reviera asks whether to open the floor over the stair's full width, and for which code's headroom:

  • the model's code (from Model Properties), listed first;
  • each other code, with its headroom and clause;
  • Don't open the floor.

The openings are vertical openings, reshaped with their grips. To check a stair again later, select it and use Modify | Stairs › Stair Openings: it makes the offer again where headroom is still short, or says the stair has enough headroom everywhere. stair.headroom_check, stair.create_openings

In the Manor, the main stair opens the ceiling and the Level 2 floor over it for the IRC's 6 ft 8 in.

Spiral stairs

  1. Open a plan. Architecture › Circulation › Spiral Stair. Spiral Stair
  2. Click the centre, then a point on the walking line (its radius).

The spiral climbs to the level above with full steps, and offers to open the floor over it like any stair (codes give spiral stairs their own headroom). The Manor's tower has two.

Stair path, cut mark and tread numbers

Plans draw each stair's path through the middles of its treads with an arrow, labelled UP on the level it starts from (DN on the level it reaches); where a stair passes the plan's cut plane, the path stops and a zig-zag cut mark crosses the tread. To number the treads in a view, select stairs and click Modify | Stairs › Number Treads: the numbers follow the treads' order along the stair, so they stay when the stair moves. stair.number_treads
