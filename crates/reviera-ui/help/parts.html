Parts

Parts break an element into the pieces that are actually built: each layer of a wall, then panels, pours or sheets of a layer. They can be scheduled, tagged and given their own material, while the element stays the source of their shape: change the wall and its parts follow.

See it in the Editing tools sample (a layered wall in parts, divided, merged and excluded), with its walkthrough.

Creating parts

Select walls, floors, ceilings, roofs, columns or beams and click Modify › Parts › Create Parts Create Parts. A straight wall with layers gives one part per layer, its openings and corner joins included, so the parts fill the wall exactly; other elements (and single-layer walls) give one part each. Stacked, curved and curtain walls cannot have parts yet. parts.create

When the wall's type changes, its parts change with it; a part whose layer is removed from the type is deleted, and the status bar says so.

Parts Visibility

Each view shows parts (the original elements are hidden and cannot be picked — the default), the original elements (parts hidden), or both. Click Parts Visibility to choose for the active view. view.set_parts_visibility

Dividing parts

Select parts and click Divide Parts:

  • By lines: draw model lines across the parts (on the wall's face for vertical joints, in plan for a floor), select them with the parts, then Divide Parts. Each line cuts along its work plane's normal.
  • By references: with no lines selected, tick levels, grids and reference planes in the list; the cuts follow them when they move.

Type a divider gap to leave a joint between the pieces. The divided part is kept but no longer drawn; pieces can be divided again. parts.divide also takes an offset per reference.

Merge, exclude, restore, delete

Merge PartsOne part from touching parts of the same material.
Exclude PartsKeeps the parts but leaves them out of views and the IFC export.
Restore PartsBrings back the excluded parts of the selected elements (or all of them).
DeleteDeleting a piece deletes its whole division and shows the part it came from again; deleting a first part deletes all the element's parts.

A part does not move on its own: move its element. parts.merge, parts.exclude, parts.restore

Properties

A selected part shows its material (type one to override the original's; clear it for Material By Original), Construction (Core or Finish), original category and type, base level, volume and largest face area. parts.info, parts.set_overrides (also the base level). In IFC each drawn part is an IfcBuildingElementPart aggregated by its element.

Not yet available: Edit Division and Edit Merged, arcs and splines as division lines, division profiles, shape handles, phases, parts of layered floors and roofs, copying parts with their element, part schedules and tags, and writing the element without its own body in IFC.
