Doors and windows

Doors and windows are placed in walls, straight or curved, and cut their opening in the wall. Their types set the size, the shape and, for doors, how they open.

Placing Position and size Facing and hand Shapes Door operations Clearances

Placing doors and windows

  1. Architecture › Build › Door (DR) or Window (WN), in a plan or a 3D view. Door Window
  2. Optionally choose the type in Properties › Type.
  3. Hover a wall: a preview follows the cursor along it. The side of the wall the cursor is on sets the facing (for a door, the side it swings to). Spacebar flips a door's hand.
  4. Click to place it. The tool stays on for more; Esc ends it.

A new door or window fits the wall where it lands: it slides or shrinks into the free gap between its neighbours and the wall's ends, and the preview shows the fitted size. On a dormer's front wall a window rises above the main roof and shrinks to fit under the dormer roof. Each new one gets the next Mark. Plans show door swings. door.place, window.place

Position and size

Clicks place doors and windows roughly. To place one exactly, select it and edit Properties › Placement:

From Wall Start, From Wall EndThe distance of its centre along the wall from either end.
Sill HeightThe bottom above the wall's base level. It moves the door or window and keeps its size; Head Height follows.
Own Size, Width, HeightThis one's own size, in place of the type's. It stays centred.

You can also drag a selected door or window along its wall. insert.set_position, insert.set_sill_height, insert.set_size

The type's values (Properties › Type Parameters) are Width, Height, Frame Width, Frame Depth, Panel Thickness and Default Sill Height, plus the shape and the operation below. Use Duplicate Type… to make a new size; see making a door type.

Facing and hand

Flip FacingTurns the selected doors and windows to face the wall's other side (a door then swings to the other side). Modify | Doors or Modify | Windows.
Flip HandMoves the selected doors' hinges to the other side. Modify | Doors, or Spacebar while placing.

Both are also in the quick modify menu. insert.flip_facing, insert.flip_hand

Shapes

A type's Shape in Properties sets the outline; the opening in the wall, the frame and the glass all follow it.

ShapeValues
RectangleThe default.
Round, EllipticalWidth and height (a round window's width is its diameter).
Round TopHead Profile: Semicircular, Segmental, Elliptical, Gothic or Tudor. Segmental, elliptical and Tudor heads have a Rise; a Gothic head a Radius Ratio (1 is equilateral). Spring Height shows the height of the straight sides.
FanA fan light.
GableA pitched head with its Rise.
TrapezoidLeft Height and Right Height.
PolygonSides and Rotation.

Doors take Rectangle and Round Top. Windows also have Glazing Bars: a number of bars, radiating in round tops, fans and round windows and upright in the rest.

Plans cut a shaped window at its true width at the cut height. When a shaped window is fitted to a smaller gap, a round window stays round and a semicircular or Gothic head keeps its shape. The Manor has an arched door, round-top and Gothic windows and an oculus.

Door operations

A door type's Operation, Leaves and Automatic rows set how it opens:

OperationSingle Swing, Double Swing (a pair), Revolving (the width is the drum's) or Sliding (Bi-parting).
LeavesSolid or Glass (Storefront).
AutomaticA powered operator over the head.

A sliding door also claims the wall its leaves slide over, one leaf width each side: windows and other doors are kept clear of it, and its slide space is checked as a virtual volume. Plans draw each kind's symbol. The Storefront sample has a revolving door, an automatic sliding door, a pair of glass doors and an automatic storefront door.

To make an automatic sliding door

  1. Place any door and select it.
  2. Properties › Type › Duplicate Type…, named for example Automatic Sliding 2000.
  3. Under Type Parameters: Width 2000, Height 2300, Operation Sliding (Bi-parting), Leaves Glass (Storefront), Automatic Yes.

Clearances

Every door has a swing volume, and with the ADA 2010 rule set its maneuvering clearances: Check Overlaps reports what intrudes into them. See Clearances and virtual volumes.

In IFC, a door or window is an IfcDoor or IfcWindow filling an IfcOpeningElement that voids the wall; a door's operation type is written too.

Not yet available: quarter-round fan lights, ready-made shaped types in the type list (duplicate a type and set its Shape), and gate swings in plans.
