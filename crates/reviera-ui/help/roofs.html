Roofs

Pitched and flat roofs are made from a sketched footprint, with eaves that bear on the walls and dormers. Arched (barrel) roofs and domes have tools of their own.

To make a roof from a footprint

  1. Open the plan of the level the walls stand on. Architecture › Build › Roof. Roof
  2. The Create Roof Footprint sketch starts with Pick Walls: click the walls (or hover one and press Tab for the chain). Picked walls give their exterior faces, pushed out by the overhang (600 mm unless you change it first with Overhang…). You can also draw the footprint with Line or Rectangle.
  3. Optionally use Defines Slope and click footprint curves to switch each between sloping and gable. Sloping curves show a triangle.
  4. Finish Edit Mode. A roof on picked walls sits on the walls' tops, and Reviera offers to attach the walls under it so the gables fill: answer yes.

Any simple footprint works, including courtyards (an inner loop). Each roof face is a slab of the roof type's thickness, mitred to its neighbours. roof.create_footprint, roof.edit_footprint

Shape and slopes

Select the roof; Properties show:

Roof ShapeHip, Gable, Gable (rotated), Shed, Shed (reversed) or Flat. It sets which footprint edges slope.
SlopeThe slope of every slope-defining edge. Edges can have different slopes (roof.set_edge, or the curve's Slope in the sketch).
OverhangHow far the roof reaches past every footprint edge.
Base Level, Base Offset From LevelThe height the roof starts from.
Cutoff Level, Cutoff OffsetCuts the roof off flat at a height, for mansard and cut-off roofs.
TypeThe roof type and its thickness; Duplicate Type….

Area, volume and the maximum ridge height are shown too. Drag the footprint's grips in a plan, or use Modify | Roofs › Edit Footprint, to change the outline. roof.set_params, roof.set_shape, roof.set_slope

Attaching walls

Select the roof and use Modify | Roofs › Attach Walls: every wall under the roof attaches to it. Gable walls rise to the ridge and the rest meet the roof's underside. To attach single walls, see Walls. roof.attach_walls

Eaves

Select a roof and use Modify | Roofs › Eaves: its sloped edges become eaves that bear on the walls below. (A roof made from picked walls gets eaves by default.) Properties › Eaves then sets:

OverhangMeasured from the wall's exterior face.
BearingWhere the roof's underside meets the wall: Exterior Face, Core Centre or Interior Face.
Heel HeightThe roof construction's depth at the bearing line; more than the roof's own depth raises the roof.
Rafter CutPlumb, Square or Two-Cut.
Fascia, Fascia DepthA fascia board, mitred at hips.
SoffitBoxed, Sloped or Open, returning to the wall's face.

roof.set_eaves, roof.eaves

Dormers

  1. Select the roof. Modify | Roofs › Dormer.
  2. Choose the style: Gable (pitched), Hipped (pitched), Shed (flat lift) or Flat.
  3. Click on the roof where each dormer goes, in a plan or in 3D. Esc ends.

Each dormer comes with its roof, a front wall and cheek walls down to the main roof; the walls stop at the main roof's underside, so the cheek walls follow its slope. Its roof joins the main roof flush, meeting it in valleys, and the main roof is cut open below the dormer. Select a dormer's roof or walls to edit the dormer in Properties: Style, Width, Front Height, Slope and Overhang. A window placed in the front wall fits under the dormer roof. Deleting the dormer (or its roof) removes its parts. See the House. dormer.create, dormer.set, dormer.move

Arched roofs

  1. Architecture › Build › Arched Roof. Arched Roof
  2. Sketch a four-sided footprint (Pick Walls, Line or Rectangle) and finish. The arch springs from the longer pair of sides.
  3. Accept attaching the walls: the end walls fill up to the arch.

Properties › Arch: Rise, Overhang, End Overhang, Profile (Semicircle, Circular Segment, Elliptical or Catenary) and, over a trapezoid footprint, Taper (Constant Rise or Constant Slope). The Observatory's annex has one. roof.create_arched, roof.set_arch

Domes

  1. Architecture › Build › Dome. Dome
  2. Choose the plan (Circle, Ellipse, or a Square, Hexagon or Octagon cloister vault) and the profile (Hemisphere, Segmental, Pointed or Onion).
  3. In a plan, click the centre, then a point on the springing (the rim).
  4. Accept attaching the walls: a drum of walls under it rises to the dome.

Properties › Dome: Centre X, Centre Y, Radius (a hemisphere's rise follows), Sides, Rotation, Rise, the profile's Ratio, an Oculus and Faceted. See the Manor's onion dome. roof.create_dome, roof.set_dome

Roofs by extrusion

A roof by extrusion is a profile drawn in a section or elevation, carried across the view:

  1. Open a section or elevation that looks at the roof's profile (the plane of the view is the roof's plane).
  2. Click Architecture › Build › Roof by Extrusion Roof by Extrusion, then click the points of the roof's top: two lines for a gable, one for a shed, more for a mansard.
  3. Press Enter. The roof's thickness hangs below the profile, and it runs across over the walls under it (or 6 m).

Its Reference Level is the highest level at or below the profile, and it moves with it. Scripts and agents can put the profile on a grid or a named reference plane (then it follows them), and give it arcs (a barrel roof) and its Extrusion Start and End. roof.create_extrusion, roof.set_extrusion

Roofs are saved as IfcRoof with faceted bodies.
