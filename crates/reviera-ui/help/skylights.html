Skylights

Skylights are glazed units set in a roof. Each cuts its own opening, moves with its roof and is deleted with it.

Placing skylights

  1. Click Architecture › Opening › Skylight Skylight.
  2. Click a roof where the skylight's middle goes, in a plan or a 3D view. The tool repeats until Esc.

With the style left at Automatic, a pitched roof gets a roof window and a flat roof a ridge lantern. The tool's Properties choose another style, and a width and length (empty means the style's usual size).

Styles

Roof WindowFor pitched roofs: a frame and glass lying in the slope, its length up the slope and its width across it (780 × 1180 mm).
FlatFor flat roofs: a glass sheet on a curb (1.2 × 1.2 m).
PyramidFor flat roofs: four glass faces rising at the glass slope to an apex, on a curb (1.2 × 1.2 m).
Ridge LanternFor flat roofs: two glass slopes rising to a ridge along its length, with glazed gable ends, on a curb (1.5 × 3 m).

Lantern panes

A ridge lantern's panes follow its length: it has as many equal panes as keep each one no wider than its Largest Pane (900 mm), with a glazing bar between each two. Lengthen it and panes are added; shorten it and they are taken away. The read-only Panes row shows the count.

Properties

StyleAny of the styles above.
Width, LengthThe outline. A roof window's length runs up the slope.
RotationTurns a flat-roof skylight in plan (a roof window follows its slope).
Curb HeightThe curb under a flat-roof skylight (150 mm).
Glass SlopeThe slope of a pyramid's or lantern's glass (30°).
Largest PaneSee Lantern panes.

A skylight must lie within one roof face, clear of the roof's other openings: a placement or change that breaks this is refused. skylight.set

Moving and copying

Move, Copy, Rotate and Mirror work on skylights; they stay in their roof. When the roof is moved, its skylights go with it.

In IFC

A skylight is an IfcWindow (SKYLIGHT) filling an IfcOpeningElement in its roof. The opening is cut plumb through the roof under the skylight's outline.

Not yet available: openings cut square to the roof face, flashing, and skylight types in the type list.
